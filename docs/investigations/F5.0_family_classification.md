### The pipeline facts the four families hang on (verified)

`GameRoundManager._scorePipeline(cards)` runs three phases per capture:

- **Phase 1** (per card, `_scoringSpirits`): `onCardScored` → applied to points/mult
  (scaled by `count`); then `onCardSeen` → updates accumulator state (no score).
- **Phase 1.5** (per card, `run.allSpirits`): the **retrigger loop** re-runs **`onCardScored`
  only** (`GRM:1480-1507`). It does **NOT** re-run `onCardSeen` and does **NOT** re-run
  `applyEngine`. *(This is the spine of the F1/F2 split.)*
- **`_fireStackCapturedHooks`** (`GRM:1514`) fires `onStackCaptured` **before** Phase 2.
- **Phase 2** (once per capture, `run.allSpirits`): `applyEngine` → applied to points/mult.
  Not re-run by retrigger. `applyEngine` results are **not** `count`-multiplied (the spirit
  bakes its own stack scaling).

After the pipeline returns, `_addCapture` calls **Region K** (`_applyPostScoreCaptureEffects`
→ `_applyCaptureCardEffects`): `run.onCardsCaptured` (Wildlife/Plenty uniqueness tally),
`sym_caterpillar` leaf-eating, `util_symbiosis` summoning, Algae accrual, goat ki — then
`onCaptureComplete` (Glory draw) and the **capture-trigger stamp dispatcher**. All of this is
**after** the capture's score is finalized.

### The classification rule applied (stated so judgment calls are auditable)

| Family | Litmus (from the code) |
|---|---|
| **F1** Scored / re-runnable | Defines `onCardScored`. In the Phase-1 loop **and re-run by Phase-1.5 retrigger**. |
| **F2** Capture-accumulator | Score comes from `applyEngine` reading the spirit's **own internal state** (a tally maintained by `onCardSeen`/`onStackCaptured` during the capture, or by event hooks like `onBank`/`onFieldDiscard`/`onCardPlayed`/`onCardDestroyed`/Wu-Xing procs across the run). Not re-run. |
| **F3** Capture-conditional | Score comes from `applyEngine` reading **external context** at score time — the captured `cards` array, or live `run` state — with **no internal tally**. Stateless recompute. Not re-run. |
| **F4** Capture-reaction | Fires in **Region K** / `onCardsCaptured` / `onCaptureComplete` (post-score). Changes future state/roster/deck/hand/consumables — **no bearing on this capture's points/mult**. |
| **F0** Non-scoring/other | No scoring/capture hook (economy, gameplay, retrigger drivers, structural capstones, sale-time meta). |
| **DELEGATED** | Mirror/Memory — family is the runtime target's; not fixed. |

**Two refinements forced by the code, both noted inline and in §C (neither is a new scoring
family, so no premise STOP):**

1. **F2 ⇄ F4 hinge = *when* the tally fires relative to the capture's own scoring.** Radiance
   and Banner tally via `onCardSeen` **inside Phase 1** (the current capture's cards count this
   capture) → **F2**. Wildlife and Plenty tally via `run.onCardsCaptured` **after** the whole
   pipeline (the current capture's cards count only *next* capture) → **F4**, exactly as the
   brief directs, *even though* both keep a score-relevant `applyEngine` read. Same wording
   ("+X mult per unique … captured"), opposite family, because of tally timing.
2. **F3 has two data-source sub-variants** (same position, different read): cards-array
   conditionals (Horizon/Dream/Hierarchy) and live-run-state engines (Surplus/Garden/Dao/Chi/
   Tengu/Feng Shui).

**Legend.** Hooks are the `_effects[id]` keys. `{}` = empty registry entry (all logic external).
`ext:` = state maintained outside `SpiritEffects`. Logic loc: SE=`SpiritEffects.js`,
GRM=`GameRoundManager.js`, RM=`RunManager.js`. desc verb = the trigger word the
`spirits.js` `description` uses. match? Y/N/~/(–) — N = rename candidate (§A), ~ = borderline,
(–) = no captured/scored verb to judge. switch = trivial / moderate / hard / n/a (non-scoring).

## §A — Mismatches (rename targets for the cosmetic sweep)

The next brief is a `"captured"`→`"scored"` sweep. The family table says **which `"captured"`
usages to flip (the F1 per-card scorers) and which to leave** (the genuinely capture-based
F2/F3/F4 spirits, whose `"captured"` is correct). Primary targets — **F1 spirits whose
description says "captured"** (they fire per-card in the scoring loop and are re-run by the
retrigger loop, so `"scored"` is the accurate verb):

## §B — Hard-to-switch spirits (Layer-3 plumbing-spec worklist)

Each is rated `hard` because moving it between families means **relocating logic**, not
re-keying a hook. Identified only — the plumbing is a later campaign.

**Inline `card.*` mutation inside `onCardScored`** (the archetype the brief named):

- **`engine_kintaro`** — `onCardScored` does `card.edition = null` (consumes Gold) and tallies
  `goldsConsumed`, returning *null*; the score arrives later via `applyEngine`. So Kintaro is an
  F1-hook/F2-score hybrid whose per-card hook exists only to mutate card state and feed a Phase-2
  read. Relocating it has to carry both the mutation and the deferred read, and decide retrigger
  semantics (the consume is idempotent — gold is already null on re-runs). Its "home" between F1
  and F2 is exactly what Phase-6 playtest is meant to settle.
- **`engine_golden_toad`** — `onCardScored` does `card.edition = 'gold'` for up to N cards, gated
  by a per-capture counter `_captureAppliedCount` that is **reset at the top of `_scorePipeline`**
  (GRM:1293, with a special `run.allSpirits` pass so transcended copies reset too). The effect is
  call-site-coupled to that reset point and to iteration order; it returns no score (gilding scores
  indirectly via the +20 gold-edition math at GRM:1151 and via Kintaro). Moving families means
  moving the counter-reset contract.
- **`util_irrigation`** — `onCardScored` calls `addCardBonusPoints(card, …)` (a **permanent** card
  mutation) *and* returns `{addPoints}`. The dual duty (mutate-and-score) is what makes it hard, and
  is the root of the suspected double-scale in §C. A sibling of Kintaro/Toad the brief anticipated.

**Non-scoring side-effect riding `onCardScored`:**

- **`util_festival`** — generates stamp consumables in `onCardScored` (returns null). Because it
  lives in the per-card scoring hook, the side-effect fires once per ribbon **and is re-run by the
  Phase-1.5 retrigger loop** — i.e. Dew/Wish/Family/Rainbow/Echo multiply Festival's stamp output.
  Switching it to a true reaction hook (`onCaptureComplete`/Region K) would change that compounding
  and the per-ribbon cadence. Hook (F1) and family (F4) disagree; this is the load-bearing wrinkle.

**Score-read / tally split across files (deferred-tally engines):**

- **`engine_wildlife`** / **`engine_plenty`** — the uniqueness increment lives in
  `RunManager.onCardsCaptured` (fired in Region K, **after** the pipeline) while the score-bearing
  read lives in `SpiritEffects.applyEngine` (Phase 2). To make them behave like Radiance/Banner
  (current capture counts immediately, F2) the tally would have to move into Phase 1; as written
  they are F4 with a score-relevant read straddling two files. Any switchboard must reconcile the
  two sites.

**Dual data source:**

- **`engine_velocity`** — `applyEngine` combines an **internal exponential accumulator** (`1.5^t2Procs`,
  Cat-1′, the one non-linear accumulator) with a **live deck read** (iron-count `× perIronMult`). Two
  scaling models in one read; the negative-path formula is bespoke. Relocating either half is
  entangled with the other.

**Inherent call-site coupling (delegation):**

- **`game_mirror`** / **`engine_memory`** — no fixed family; they delegate to a neighbour's hooks
  (closest-left / rightmost-non-self) in true placement order, scale by `_scaleEngineOutput^n`, and
  are recursion-guarded against each other. Any family switchboard must treat them as
  pass-throughs whose "family" resolves at runtime, and preserve adjacency/order semantics.

*(Velocity aside, the other Wu Xing / event-accumulators are `moderate`: their tally hook is
separate from a clean `applyEngine` read, so re-keying is mechanical.)*

## §C — Surprises / open questions

**The four-family model HOLDS. No score-relevant fifth family exists → no premise STOP.** F0 is
populated only by genuinely non-scoring spirits (plus structural capstones and retrigger drivers,
both expected). The items below are sub-variants, hybrids, and one suspected bug — not new families.

1. **`util_irrigation` — suspected quadratic-in-stacks double-scale (LOGGED, NOT FIXED).**
   `onCardScored` computes `bonus = _tb(spirit,'points',3) × effectivePower(spirit)` (i.e. `3 ×
   stacks`), then Phase 1 multiplies the returned `addPoints` by `count = effectivePower(spirit)`
   for non-accumulators (irrigation is **not** in `ACCUMULATOR_SPIRIT_IDS`). Net immediate score =
   `3 × stacks²`. Trace: a **2-stack** Irrigation scores **+12** per plain immediately, while the
   *permanent* mutation `addCardBonusPoints(card, 6)` adds **+6** — so the immediate bonus is
   **double** the permanent one, contradicting the F2.1.c intent ("addPoints matches the permanent
   mutation's stack scaling", DECISIONS_LOG 2026-05-15). Irrigation is the **only** `onCardScored`
   spirit that both bakes `effectivePower` into its return *and* is `count`-multiplied (every other
   F1 spirit returns a per-stack value and relies on `count` alone). Almost certainly a regression
   introduced when the `ACCUMULATOR_IDS ? 1 : effectivePower` count-generalization swept irrigation
   into the scaled branch; no irrigation **stacking** test exists to have caught it. Code-trace
   confident; recommend empirical confirmation + fix in a dedicated [FIX] campaign (this is an
   "F5.12-style stacking inconsistency" of the kind §5 says to log here). **Not fixed.**

2. **Festival: a non-scoring side-effect on the F1 hook.** (Detailed in §B.) The only spirit where
   `onCardScored` is used purely for a reaction (stamp generation, no points/mult), which silently
   subjects that reaction to the retrigger loop. Whatever vocabulary/switch model F5.0 adopts has to
   account for "F4-by-effect, F1-by-hook." Listed F4 here by effect; flagged so it isn't mistaken
   for a scorer.

3. **F2 ⇄ F4 turns on tally *timing*, not wording.** Radiance/Banner (`onCardSeen`, Phase 1 → F2)
   and Wildlife/Plenty (`run.onCardsCaptured`, Region K, post-score → F4) share the phrase "+X mult
   per unique … captured" but land in opposite families. Verified consequence: a freshly-captured
   unique animal does **not** boost the capture that caught it (Wildlife reads prior state; the new
   animal is tallied *after* scoring) — whereas a fresh bright **does** boost its own capture under
   Radiance. The brief's split is correct; this is the mechanical reason, and it means
   Wildlife/Plenty are "F4 with a score-relevant `applyEngine` read," not pure reactions.

4. **`sym_algae` shares Wildlife/Plenty's shape but is classified F2.** Algae's `summonCount`
   increment also fires in Region K (the symbiosis-summon block, GRM:1678), and its `applyEngine`
   read is score-relevant — structurally identical to Wildlife/Plenty. It is classified **F2** here
   because (a) it is a canonical accumulator (`ACCUMULATOR_IDS`, `NEGATIVE_SNAPSHOT` Cat-1 linear)
   and (b) the brief's F4 reference list names **Symbiosis** (the summoner) but not Algae (the
   scorer). **Open question for Robert:** apply the "classify by where the increment fires" rule
   consistently (→ Algae F4 alongside Wildlife/Plenty), or treat Algae as a scorer (→ F2, as here)?
   The two are otherwise the same machine.

5. **Kintaro is a genuine F1/F2 hybrid.** Its score is an `applyEngine` `multiplyMult` (F2-shape),
   but its defining, problematic hook is the card-mutating `onCardScored` (F1). This is consistent
   with the brief's explicit deferral of Kintaro/Golden Toad's "correct home" to Phase-6 — the
   switch-difficulty audit confirms the swap is `hard`, which is the data point that deferral needs.

6. **F3 is not monolithic — two data-source sub-variants** (same retrigger-loop position, different
   read): **cards-array conditionals** (Horizon/Dream/Hierarchy) and **live-run-state engines**
   (Surplus/Garden/Dao/Chi/Tengu/Feng Shui). The brief's F3 definition ("reads the captured `cards`
   array") literally covers only the first; the second reads `run.ki`/`getDeck()`/`flow`/spirit-count/
   slots instead. Same family by position (Phase-2 `applyEngine`, stateless recompute, not re-run);
   noted so the vocabulary/switch work treats them together but knows they differ in input. (Per §C
   guidance this is a sub-variant, not an invented scoring family.)

7. **F2 spans accumulators *and* non-accumulator internal-state engines.** Beyond the canonical
   `ACCUMULATOR_IDS` accumulators, F2 also holds **Ducks** (`multValue`, deck-flip-driven) and the
   two **decay** spirits (`remaining`, decremented each round — *decaying*, not accumulating). The
   F2 label "capture-accumulator" is slightly imprecise for these; they sit in F2 by position
   (Phase-2 `applyEngine` reading internal state, not re-run).

8. **Capstones reshape scoring structurally without any hook.** Yin-Yang (fire-each-slot-twice),
   Universe (mirror mult→points), and Nature (carry points across captures) are implemented as
   inline flags in `_scorePipeline` (their `_effects` entries are `{}`); Time touches flow in
   RunManager. They are F0 by the hook model but are **not** inert — they are a scoring-structural
   legendary layer **outside** the four-family (chain-hook) taxonomy. Worth stating explicitly so
   the model isn't read as "F0 = no scoring impact."

9. **Retrigger drivers are F0 but scoring-adjacent.** Rainbow/Family/Wish/Dew/Echo (`'capture'`) and
   Applause (`'held_in_hand'`) expose only `getRetriggerCount` — they **drive** the Phase-1.5 loop
   that re-runs F1, but score nothing themselves. They are F0 (no scoring hook), but functionally
   distinct from inert economy F0. Expected per the brief (it frames retriggers as the loop); noted
   for completeness.

10. **Capture-trigger stamp dispatcher (context, not a spirit — per §3).** The yellow/orange/green/
    black/gray captured-stamp effects (GRM:1599-1613) share the retrigger loop with F1 via
    `_computeRetriggerCount(card, 'capture', …)`, i.e. the same `'capture'` trigger type that the
    2026-05-15 merge unified with scoring math ("scoring + capture-trigger stamps fire in one atomic
    capture event"). This is the established reason the vocabulary question ("scored" vs "captured")
    is entangled, and the reason Festival's stamp side-effect (item 2) compounds with retriggers. Not
    in the spirit table; recorded as the backdrop for the vocabulary decision.
